      AIARCADE

      A store of classic arcade games, rebuilt for the browser with AI opponents and helpers. Made by EVOKNOW ·

      Built with

      • Design inspiration from Kabir, engineered by his AI Factory — every game, engine and AI in this arcade came out of that pipeline.
      • HTML5 Canvas and Web Audio — all sound is synthesized live, no samples.
      • ffmpeg.wasm for in-browser MP4 clip export (FFmpeg: LGPL/GPL; libx264: GPL).
      • Typefaces Chakra Petch and Space Grotesk via Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

      Art

      • Tower Defence and Scorched Ridge sprites from Kenney — Tower Defense (top-down), released under CC0. kenney.nl
      • All other artwork, covers and icons are drawn procedurally by the games themselves.
